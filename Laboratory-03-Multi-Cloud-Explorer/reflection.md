# Mission Reflection

During this mission, the cloud platform that impressed me the most was **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**. I was impressed by the wide range of services AWS provides, from virtual machines and storage to databases, networking, serverless computing, and artificial intelligence. I also learned that AWS has a large global infrastructure that allows organizations to build applications that can scale as their needs increase.

AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform have many similarities. All three provide cloud computing services such as virtual machines, object storage, databases, networking, identity management, and Kubernetes. They also provide global infrastructure, security features, management consoles, and scalable resources that organizations can use without maintaining all of their own physical hardware.

Business requirements strongly influence cloud platform selection because different organizations have different technical and operational needs. For example, a company that already uses Microsoft products may benefit more from Azure, while an AI research company may prefer GCP because of its AI and machine learning capabilities. A startup may prioritize scalability and cost, while a global business may prioritize availability, performance, and worldwide infrastructure.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud platform is not simply about selecting the most popular provider. I learned to analyze the client's requirements, compare available services, consider advantages and limitations, and recommend a platform that best fits the organization's goals.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has also improved significantly after completing this mission. I added research on AWS, Azure, and GCP, service comparison tables, client recommendations, a decision matrix, Linux server investigation results, and screenshots as evidence of my work. This makes my portfolio more organized and demonstrates my understanding of multi-cloud concepts and practical cloud decision-making.
